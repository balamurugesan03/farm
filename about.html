Who We Are

A Kerala family of enterprises, rooted in culture and built for tomorrow.

Sreechandhinees Group of Companies is a diversified organization based in the lush heart of Kerala, honoring our cultural heritage while building sustainable and modern enterprises.

Today, we span multiple industries reflecting Kerala’s spirit of resilience, creativity, and prosperity. Every venture is more than just a business — it is a living expression of our values, our heritage, and our commitment to a better future.
