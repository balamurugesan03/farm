03

Sreechandhinees Temple Trust

Sivam Foundation Thapovanam · Sree Dakshinamoorthi Vidyapeeth — Spiritual University

Dakshinamurthy Vidyapeeth is a Shivalinga-shaped spiritual university established under the auspices of Kodakara Vattekkad Sree Dakshinamurthy Navagraha Temple, under the guidance of Acharya Brahmasree Aswini Deva Tantri. Once completed, it will be the largest Shivalinga in the world, rising 144 feet.

The Vedas, Puranas, Upanishads, Shastras, Art and Sanskrit are taught here in the gurukula tradition — carrying forward the spiritual wisdom of great souls like Adi Shankara and Sree Narayana Deva, in harmony with nature, for the well-being and happiness of all living beings.
